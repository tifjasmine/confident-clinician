## Content rules

- Use the primary phrase naturally in the title, description, H1 or opening paragraph, and at least one relevant internal link.
- Write for psychotherapists and counselors explicitly so search engines do not confuse the content with physical, occupational, or speech therapy.
- Link every article to one related article and one appropriate next step.
- Avoid creating multiple articles that answer the same question with slightly different wording.
- Prioritize concrete searches that reveal a moment of need over broad phrases such as “therapist training.”
