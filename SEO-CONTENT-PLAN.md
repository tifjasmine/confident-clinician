## Article standard

- Answer the stated question early; do not bury the useful material beneath a long introduction.
- Use a concrete clinical moment, while protecting confidentiality and avoiding individualized treatment advice.
- Include Tiffany’s byline, credentials, publication/update date, and professional-education disclaimer.
- Add one contextual link to an existing article, one related-content link, and one fitting next step.
- Give each article a unique title, description, canonical URL, H1, social preview, and Article structured data.
- Avoid publishing near-duplicate articles to chase variations of the same phrase.
- Review articles at least annually for accuracy, usefulness, broken links, and alignment with current offerings.
