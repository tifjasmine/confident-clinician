Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Tiffany McGilloway.

Licensed Professional Counselor. MS in Mental Health Counseling. Founder of The Confident Clinician and The Embodied Self.

And someone who has been exactly where you are - Googling "is this normal?" at 11pm.

The Real Story

I know the "one more training will fix this" loop.

I have been the clinician searching for one more training, one more course, one more explanation that would finally make the work feel less shaky. Some I finished. Some I started with the best intentions. All of it came from wanting to feel more prepared for the real moments that happen with real clients.

I have also been in the late-night search spiral, typing questions like "How do I even respond to this?" "Why am I still thinking about that session?" "What do I say when a client shuts down?" and "How do I market myself without feeling fake?" I created The Confident Clinician for therapists who are caring, capable, and still trying to find their footing in the parts of the work no one explains clearly enough.

Over the years, I've learned that many newer clinicians are not lacking passion, intelligence, or care. They're lacking a bridge.

A bridge between theory and real sessions.
A bridge between training and confidence.
A bridge between "I know the information"
and "I know how to be with this person in front of me."

My work blends clinical clarity, nervous system awareness, self-trust, identity, and embodied presence - because the therapist is not separate from the work.

My Practice

Visit theembodiedself.me Book a Session

The Embodied Self is my private practice. I work with women who are tired of carrying everything alone - the anxiety, the burnout, the feeling that they should have it more together by now - and are ready to build a more honest, compassionate relationship with themselves.

I blend traditional talk therapy with body-based, mindfulness-informed approaches - because I've seen how much healing gets missed when we only work from the neck up.

You are not sitting across from a blank wall. You are working with a real person who brings warmth, creativity, groundedness, curiosity, and a little playfulness into the room.

What I Believe About This Work

You are not behind.

The uncertainty you feel is not a flaw. It is a sign you care. What you need is a clearer foundation, not more pressure on top of an already full plate.

The therapist is not separate from the work.

Clinical clarity, nervous system awareness, self-trust, and identity are not soft extras. They are the work. Your presence in that room matters as much as your technique.

Sustainability is the goal, not the reward.

A clinician who burns out cannot help anyone. Building a practice that is honest, boundaried, and sustainable is not selfish. It is the foundation everything else rests on.
