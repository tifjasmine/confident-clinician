How practice works

Practice that talks back.

Here’s a peek at one kind of practice inside the Lab: a live session with a fictional client who reacts to your exact words. After each response you get a training note on what landed and one thing to build on, then you decide your next move.

ClientEveryone keeps telling me I'm overreacting.

YouI don't think you're overreacting at all!

Client…Okay.

You, second passIt sounds like you keep hearing that your reaction is too much. What’s that been like for you?

ClientHonestly? Lonely. Like nobody’s actually listening.

Training noteWhat’s working: You slowed down and stayed with her experience instead of rushing to reassure her. Asking what it’s been like gave her room to answer, and she told you something real.
Build on it: “Lonely” is the heart of what she just gave you. Stay curious about that word before you move anywhere else.
