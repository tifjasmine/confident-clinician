A Workshop for Newer Therapists

A grounded workshop for newer therapists

For interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and early-career therapists who want steadier session presence and a clearer clinical voice.

Sometimes it can feel like…

  • "Am I doing this right?"
  • "What do I say next?"
  • "Why do I feel so unsure when I technically know what I'm doing?"

We'll slow the spiral, organize the overwhelm, and help you leave with a usable next step.

The Real Problem

You're not underqualified.
You're under-supported.

Many newer clinicians leave school with plenty of care and information, but not enough practice translating that into their own way of being in the room. You may still feel unsure about:

  • How to choose an intervention in the moment
  • How to stop comparing yourself to more seasoned clinicians
  • How to structure sessions without sounding robotic
  • How to trust your instincts without feeling like you're "making it up"
  • How to explain your approach to clients

This workshop helps you zoom out and build a clearer way to think, respond, and show up.

Who This Is For

This is for the clinician who is still finding their voice.

✦Interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and newer therapists

✦Clinicians who feel like they learned theories but not how to apply them naturally

✦Therapists who feel anxious before or after sessions

✦Clinicians who second-guess their interventions

✦Therapists who want to find their style instead of copying someone else's

For the therapist who has something meaningful to offer and wants help connecting the dots.

The Promise

Leave with clearer language and a stronger sense of your next step.

The goal is not to make you a perfect therapist. It is to help you feel less scattered and more able to use what you already know.

The Framework

A clearer way to grow into your clinical voice

This workshop introduces the foundation of the CLEAR process - a professional development framework for organizing how you think, speak, and show up as a clinician.

We will not cram every therapy model into one workshop. We will focus on building an internal map you can actually use.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this clinical supervision?

No. This workshop is professional development and clinician confidence support. It is not a replacement for clinical supervision, consultation, therapy, or legal/ethical guidance from your board, supervisor, or workplace.

Is this only for private practice clinicians?

No. This workshop can support newer clinicians in agency, school, group practice, community, and private practice settings. The focus is on clinical confidence, clarity, and identity — not just business building.

Do I need to know my niche already?

No. This workshop is actually a great place to begin exploring what feels aligned before forcing yourself into a niche too early.

Will this teach me one specific therapy model?

No. This is not a model-specific training. Instead, it helps you begin understanding how to think about therapy styles, clinical presence, and your own way of working more clearly.

Is this for established clinicians?

This is best for newer clinicians, interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and therapists who are still finding their voice. If you already feel very established and clear in your approach, this may not be the best fit.

Will there be a replay?

Details will be shared when registration opens.
