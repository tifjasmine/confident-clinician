The Confident Clinician Workshops

For the things therapy school did not fully teach you.

Short, focused trainings for therapists who are ready to turn everything they have learned into a clearer, steadier way of working.

Sometimes it can feel like...

"Am I doing this right?"

"What do I say next?"

"Should I be using CBT, ACT, DBT, somatic work, inner child work, or something else?"

"Why do I feel so unsure when I technically know what I'm doing?"

These grounded workshops were created to help you stop spiraling, stop over-consuming trainings, and start building a grounded clinical voice you can actually trust.

The Real Problem

You're not underqualified.
You're under-supported.

So many newer clinicians leave school with a degree, a diagnosis manual, a few therapy models, and a deep desire to help people - but not always a clear sense of how to actually be themselves in the room. You may know the textbook version of therapy, but still feel unsure about:

How to choose an intervention in the moment

How to stop comparing yourself to more seasoned clinicians

How to structure sessions without sounding robotic

How to trust your instincts without feeling like you're "making it up"

How to blend different therapy styles ethically

How to market yourself without feeling fake, pushy, or outside your values

How to explain your approach to clients

This workshop is here to help you breathe, zoom out, and start building the kind of confidence that does not come from knowing everything - but from having a clearer way to think, respond, and show up.

Who This Is For

This is for the clinician who is still finding their voice.

  • Interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and newer therapists
  • Clinicians who feel like they learned theories but not how to apply them naturally
  • Therapists who feel anxious before or after sessions
  • Clinicians who second-guess their interventions
  • Therapists who want to find their style instead of copying someone else's
  • Clinicians who want to feel more grounded, ethical, and human in their work
  • Therapists beginning to think about their niche, messaging, or private practice identity
  • Clinicians who are tired of collecting trainings but still not feeling confident

The Promise

Leave with more clarity, more language, and a stronger sense of yourself as a clinician.

The goal of this workshop is not to make you a perfect therapist. The goal is to help you feel less scattered and more grounded.

Inside the workshop, we'll look at what actually builds clinician confidence: not just more information, but clearer thinking, stronger clinical language, embodied presence, and a deeper understanding of how you want to work.

The Framework

A clearer way to grow into your clinical voice

This workshop introduces the foundation of the CLEAR process - a professional development framework designed to help newer clinicians move from scattered, anxious, and unsure to more grounded, intentional, and self-trusting.

We won't be cramming every therapy model into one workshop. Instead, we'll focus on helping you create a clearer internal map for how you think, speak, and show up as a clinician.

Because confidence is not just about learning more. It's about knowing how to use what you already know.

You will not walk away knowing every intervention for every client. You will walk away with more clarity around how to begin trusting yourself.

Over the years, I've learned that many newer clinicians are not lacking passion, intelligence, or care. They're lacking a bridge.

A bridge between theory and real sessions.
A bridge between training and confidence.
A bridge between "I know the information" and "I know how to be with this person in front of me."

My work blends clinical clarity, nervous system awareness, self-trust, identity, and embodied presence - because the therapist is not separate from the work.

What Comes Next

The 12-week program is where this work gets practiced, personalized, and integrated.

If the workshop helps you name what has felt scattered, the 12-week program gives you a longer space to build the way you actually want to think, speak, market, set boundaries, and show up as a clinician.

Session Clarity

Practice thinking through real clinical moments without reaching for a script, over-explaining, or abandoning your own judgment.

Your Clinical Lens

Sort through the models, trainings, instincts, and lived experience that shape your work so your approach starts to sound like you.

Boundaries & Repair

Work with endings, cancellations, feedback, client discomfort, and the moments where warmth and structure both matter.

Reflection Without Spiraling

Build a way to review sessions, notes, consultation, and hard moments without turning every uncertainty into proof you are failing.

Ethical Visibility

Connect your clinical identity to messaging, niche, and marketing language that feels grounded, honest, and aligned with your values.

Sustainable Confidence

Create practices for carrying the work with more steadiness, more self-trust, and less pressure to become a perfect therapist.

The deeper program is for clinicians who want more than reassurance. It is for building a clinical voice you can keep growing into.
