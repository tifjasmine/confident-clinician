Who This Is For

This is for the clinician who wants real-world support, not more pressure to sound perfect.

✦Interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and newer therapists

✦Therapists who feel anxious before, during, or after sessions

✦Clinicians who want scripts without becoming scripted

✦Therapists who replay sessions and wonder if they did enough

✦Clinicians who want to be ethical, boundaried, human, and grounded

Clinical confidence is not never doubting yourself. It is knowing how to come back to yourself.

Featured Workshop

When you know the theory, but still freeze in the room.

This is the starting workshop for therapists who want help with the moment-by-moment uncertainty of session: what to notice, what to say, what to hold, and how to return to your role when self-doubt gets loud.

Inside the Featured Workshop

What you'll begin practicing

01How to slow down when you feel the urge to perform, fix, or fill the space

02How to name what is happening without making every pause a problem

03How to use simple grounding questions when your mind goes blank

04How to stay warm and boundaried when the frame gets hard to hold

05How to review sessions without turning reflection into a shame spiral

06How to trust steadiness, curiosity, and clinical information - not just interventions

A useful place to pause and sort things out - not another training that leaves you with 100 things to memorize.

The Framework

A simple way to think through your work

The featured workshop introduces the foundation of the CLEAR process - a framework for noticing what is happening, choosing your next step, and talking about your work more naturally.

Each workshop can return to the same simple question: what is happening here, what is mine to hold, and what would be a grounded next step?

Your Takeaways

By the end of the workshop,
you'll walk away with:

A better understanding of why certain moments make you freeze or over-function

Language for silence, uncertainty, boundaries, and clinical repair

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this clinical supervision?

No. These workshops are professional development for clinicians. They are not a replacement for supervision, consultation, therapy, or legal/ethical guidance from your board, supervisor, or workplace.

Is this only for private practice clinicians?

No. These workshops can support clinicians in agency, school, group practice, community, and private practice settings. The focus is on how you think and show up in the work, not just business building.

Are these self-paced?

Some workshops may be available to move through on your own time. Details will be shared as each topic opens.

Will this teach me one specific therapy model?

No. These are not model-specific trainings. They help you think about clinical presence, language, boundaries, reflection, and your own way of working.

Is this for established clinicians?

This is best for newer clinicians, interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and therapists who are still finding their voice. If you already feel very established and clear in your approach, this may not be the best fit.

Will there be a replay?

Details will be shared when registration opens.

You do not have to figure it all out alone.

Feeling unsure does not mean you are not meant for this work. It usually means you need more space to practice thinking like yourself.
