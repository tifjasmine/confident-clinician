A Workshop for Newer Therapists

When you know the theory, but still freeze in the room.

A workshop for interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and early-career therapists who want practical help making sense of what to do next in session.

Sometimes it can feel like…

  • "Am I doing this right?"
  • "What do I say next?"
  • "Why do I feel so unsure when I technically know what I'm doing?"

We'll look at the moments that make you second-guess yourself and give you a simple way to think through them.

The Real Problem

You're not underqualified.
You're under-supported.

Many newer clinicians leave school with care, information, and a stack of therapy models, but not enough practice deciding what actually fits in the moment. You may still feel unsure about:

  • How to choose an intervention in the moment
  • How to stop comparing yourself to more seasoned clinicians
  • How to structure sessions without sounding robotic
  • How to trust your instincts without feeling like you're "making it up"
  • How to explain your approach to clients

This workshop gives you a way to slow down, sort through the noise, and make more thoughtful clinical choices.

Who This Is For

This is for the clinician who is still finding their footing.

✦Interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and newer therapists

✦Clinicians who learned theories but want help applying them naturally

✦Therapists who feel anxious before or after sessions

✦Clinicians who second-guess their interventions

✦Therapists who want to sound like themselves, not like a script

For the therapist who cares deeply and wants more support connecting the dots.

The Promise

Leave with language you can actually use.

The goal is not to make you a perfect therapist. It is to help you feel more prepared for the real questions that come up in session.

The Framework

A simple way to think through your work

This workshop introduces the foundation of the CLEAR process - a framework for noticing what is happening, choosing your next step, and talking about your work more naturally.

We will not cram every therapy model into one workshop. We will focus on a map you can come back to when you feel unsure.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this clinical supervision?

No. This workshop is professional development for newer clinicians. It is not a replacement for supervision, consultation, therapy, or legal/ethical guidance from your board, supervisor, or workplace.

Is this only for private practice clinicians?

No. This workshop can support newer clinicians in agency, school, group practice, community, and private practice settings. The focus is on how you think and show up in the work, not just business building.

Do I need to know my niche already?

No. This workshop is a good place to begin noticing what feels aligned before forcing yourself into a niche too early.

Will this teach me one specific therapy model?

No. This is not a model-specific training. It helps you think about therapy styles, clinical presence, and your own way of working.

Is this for established clinicians?

This is best for newer clinicians, interns, provisionally licensed clinicians, and therapists who are still finding their voice. If you already feel very established and clear in your approach, this may not be the best fit.

Will there be a replay?

Details will be shared when registration opens.

You do not have to figure it all out alone.

Feeling unsure does not mean you are not meant for this work. It usually means you need more space to practice thinking like yourself.

If you're a newer clinician who wants a better starting point, this workshop is for you.
