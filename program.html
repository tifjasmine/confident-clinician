12-Week Professional Development Program

The 12-Week Fast Track

A structured professional development program for newer therapists who want to clarify their style, strengthen their clinical thinking, and practice with more steadiness.

Join the Founding Cohort Waitlist

Who This Is For

This is for the clinician who wants integration, not another pile of notes.

It is for the clinician who wants support understanding how they think clinically, how they speak about their work, and how they stay present when sessions feel uncertain.

The Fast Track helps newer clinicians move from scattered and unsure to clearer, more intentional, and more practiced.

The Program

What We'll Build Together

Clinical Clarity

Understand what you notice in sessions and how you make sense of what clients bring in.

Therapeutic Voice

Develop language for your style, your approach, and the way you support clients.

Session Presence

Practice feeling less frozen or performative when you are in the room.

Ethical Integration

Blend your training, interests, and clinical lens with clearer boundaries.

Niche & Messaging

Clarify who you serve, how you help, and how to speak about your work.

Reflective Practice

Learn how to review, adjust, and grow without spiraling after sessions.

This is not:

  • Clinical supervision or a replacement for board-approved consultation
  • A diagnosis or treatment planning certification
  • A rigid therapy model you must follow
  • A business-only coaching program
  • A space where you have to pretend you already feel confident

This is:

  • A structured confidence-building program
  • A professional development space
  • A place to clarify your clinical identity
  • A bridge between theory and practice
  • A supportive container for newer clinicians finding their voice
