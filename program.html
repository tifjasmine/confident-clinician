The Curriculum

Everything you wish had been covered in grad school.

Week 01

Why Confidence Feels Shaky Early On

We separate skill gaps from confidence gaps, name what's actually blocking you, and give you permission to be uncertain without treating it as proof you're failing.

Week 02

The Therapist Nervous System

Fight, flight, freeze, and fawn show up in clinical work too. Learn to recognize your activation patterns and build a grounding practice that actually holds.

Week 03

The CLEAR Method and Clinical Decision-Making

The CLEAR method gives you three questions to ask yourself in any session moment: what am I noticing, what does the client need, and what is my role right now.

Week 04

Session Structure and Flow

Open, explore, intervene, close. We map your current session habits, find where you lose direction, and practice more intentional openings and closings.

Week 05

Case Conceptualization and Treatment Thread

The 5-Lens Clinical Clarity Map helps you see the pattern beneath the problem and build a treatment thread that gives sessions real direction over time.

Week 06

Modalities Without the Overwhelm

Modalities are maps, not identities you have to perfectly perform. We cover the major approaches, when each fits, and how to blend them ethically without losing your footing.

Week 07

Difficult Session Moments and Complex Clients

Countertransference, resistance, emotional flooding, complex presentations. We work through what gets activated in you and how to stay grounded and ethical when sessions get hard.

Week 08

Boundaries, Rupture, Repair, and Client Feedback

Time, communication, fees, cancellations - plus rupture scripts, repair language, and how to hold client disappointment without caving or over-apologizing.

Week 09

Ethics, Risk, Documentation, and Consultation

Risk assessment without panic, documentation that actually protects you, ethical decision-making in real time, and knowing when to consult rather than sit alone with uncertainty.

Week 10

Burnout Prevention Beyond Self-Care

Compassion fatigue, vicarious trauma, and burnout are not the same thing. We audit the real sources - caseload, acuity, emotional over-responsibility - and build a sustainability plan that goes way past self-care advice.

Week 11

Self-Advocacy, Workplace Reality, Money, and Sustainability

W2 vs. 1099, caseload boundaries, workplace red flags, fee conversations, and how to advocate for yourself without feeling like you're being difficult.

Week 12

Clinical Identity, Professional Presence, and Integration

Your clinical voice statement, your therapeutic philosophy, your 90-day growth plan. We close by naming who you are as a clinician - not perfectly, but honestly.
