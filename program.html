12-Week Professional Development Program

Build confidence for the parts of the work no one fully prepared you for.

A deeper professional development space for therapists who want to feel more grounded in the room, clearer in their clinical voice, and less alone in the messy middle of becoming.

Join the Founding Cohort Waitlist

Who This Is For

This is for the clinician who wants support becoming grounded, not just more information to memorize.

It is for the therapist who has training and care, but still wants support with silence, boundaries, feedback, documentation anxiety, emotional hangovers, client cancellations, comparison, and the pressure to always know what to say.

The 12-week program gives clinicians a place to slow down, practice reflection, ask better questions, and build a clinical voice that is ethical, boundaried, human, and their own.

The Program

What we'll work through together

Clinical Presence

Practice what to do when you freeze, over-talk, rush to fix, or do not know what to say next.

The Therapeutic Frame

Work with session endings, cancellations, boundaries, consistency, warmth, and containment.

Your Clinical Voice

Find language that sounds like you while staying ethical, grounded, and professional.

Feedback & Consultation

Learn how to stay curious when feedback stings, supervision feels personal, or a case needs support.

Documentation & Over-Responsibility

Write and reflect with clarity without trying to prove your worth in every note.

Sustainability

Explore burnout, emotional hangovers, advocacy, workplace expectations, and what is actually yours to carry.

This is not:

  • Clinical supervision or a replacement for board-approved consultation
  • A diagnosis or treatment planning certification
  • A rigid therapy model you must follow
  • A niche, marketing, or business coaching program
  • A space where you have to pretend you already feel confident

This is:

  • A structured professional development program
  • A space for honest reflection, practice, and clinical language
  • A place to build boundaries, presence, and self-trust
  • A bridge between theory and the real moments in the room
  • A supportive place for clinicians finding their voice
