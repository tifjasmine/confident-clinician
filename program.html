12-Week Professional Development Program

The 12-Week Fast Track

A longer-form program for newer therapists who want more structure, practice, and support as they find their way in the work.

Join the Founding Cohort Waitlist

Who This Is For

This is for the clinician who wants practice, not just more information.

It is for the therapist who wants support thinking through sessions, talking about their work, and building a style that feels honest and ethical.

The Fast Track gives newer clinicians a place to slow down, ask better questions, and keep practicing between trainings and supervision.

The Program

What We'll Build Together

Clinical Thinking

Notice what you pay attention to in sessions and how you make sense of what clients bring in.

Your Way of Working

Find language for your style, your approach, and the way you support clients.

Session Presence

Practice feeling less frozen or performative when you are in the room.

Ethical Integration

Bring together your training and interests without drifting outside your role or scope.

Niche & Language

Clarify who you serve, how you help, and how to talk about your work simply.

Reflection

Learn how to review, adjust, and grow without picking yourself apart after sessions.

This is not:

  • Clinical supervision or a replacement for board-approved consultation
  • A diagnosis or treatment planning certification
  • A rigid therapy model you must follow
  • A business-only coaching program
  • A space where you have to pretend you already feel confident

This is:

  • A structured professional development program
  • A professional development space
  • A place to understand your clinical identity
  • A bridge between theory and practice
  • A supportive place for newer clinicians finding their voice
