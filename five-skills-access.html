You’re In

The 5 Skills That Separate New Therapists from Confident Clinicians

Inside this workshop, you’ll begin clarifying how to:

  1. Stop Performing Therapy and shift from proving you’re good to actually understanding the person in front of you.
  2. Tolerate Not Knowing so you can stay grounded when the next step is not immediately clear.
  3. Trust the Process Over the Perfect Intervention and remember that clients need presence, not performance.
  4. Regulate Yourself First so your nervous system is not running the session.
  5. Build Self-Trust instead of replaying every session as proof you missed something.
