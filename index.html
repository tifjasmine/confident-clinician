Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Tiffany McGilloway -
and I built this because I needed it too.

Read My Story

I'm a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) with years of experience working alongside individuals in some of their most vulnerable moments - from crisis stabilization and higher levels of care to school-based counseling and private practice.

I have been the clinician who left a session and immediately texted my supervisor "was that okay?" I have refreshed my email waiting for a complaint that never came. I have rewritten the same progress note four times because none of them felt accurate enough. All while being genuinely good at this work.

So I built the thing I kept looking for - a space that takes newer clinicians seriously and gives them real, usable tools for the work they're already doing.

What I believe

Newer clinicians don't need more information - they need someone who actually gets what the first years feel like.

What we practice

Turning theory into language, boundaries, case clarity, self-trust, and steadier choices in the moments that feel messy, quiet, or unclear.

What I've built

The Confident Clinician is where I show up for clinicians. The Embodied Self is where I show up for clients - specifically women ready to stop surviving and start feeling at home in themselves.
