Professional Development for Newer Therapists

Build the clinical confidence school didn't fully teach you.

Grad school gave you the theory. Supervision gave you feedback. But somewhere between the DSM and your first solo session, nobody covered the midnight replays, the boundary you kept meaning to hold, or the client who caught you off guard and you've been thinking about ever since.

Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Tiffany McGilloway, and I know what it's like to question yourself in the work.

I'm a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) with years of experience working alongside people in some of their most vulnerable moments, including crisis stabilization, higher levels of care, school based counseling, and private practice.

Imposter syndrome is real!!! I know what it is to leave a session replaying one sentence, wonder if a boundary landed too harshly, stare at a blank progress note because the work felt bigger than the checkbox, and still wonder if I was confident enough, skilled enough, or actually cut out for this job.

So I built the thing I kept looking for: a space that takes newer clinicians seriously and gives them real, usable tools for the work they're already doing.

I believe that

Newer clinicians don't need more theories. They need intentional practice, real support, and a clear way to turn what they already know into work they can trust in the room.
