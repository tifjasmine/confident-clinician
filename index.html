Professional Development for Newer Therapists

Feel more confident in the therapy room.

You already care deeply about the work. Now you need support for the moments no one really prepares you for: client silence, ending on time, not knowing what to say, feeling anxious in session, and carrying the work home. The Confident Clinician helps newer therapists build a grounded clinical voice that feels honest, steady, and like their own.

Therapy school gives you a lot of information.

But it does not always prepare you for the moments that happen in real time: when the room goes quiet, when a client gives hard feedback, when you are not sure what to say next, or when you leave session wondering if you did enough.

The Confident Clinician is for that space between knowing the theory and learning how to trust yourself in the room.

Created By

Tiffany Wright, LPC

Founder of The Embodied Self

I have been the clinician searching for one more training, one more course, one more explanation that would finally make the work feel less shaky. Some I finished. Some I started with the best intentions. All of it came from wanting to feel more prepared for the real moments that happen with real clients.

I have also been in the late-night search spiral, typing questions like, "Is this normal?" "Why am I still thinking about that session?" "What do I say when a client shuts down?" and "How do I market myself without feeling fake?" I created The Confident Clinician for therapists who are caring, capable, and still trying to find their footing in the parts of the work no one explains clearly enough.
