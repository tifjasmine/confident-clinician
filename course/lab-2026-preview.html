How the Lab works

You will learn a concept, make a clinical decision, compare possible responses, reflect on what you noticed, and try again.

A note about clinical confidence

The goal is not to memorize perfect lines, eliminate uncertainty, or perform confidence. The goal is to recognize what is happening clinically, make an informed choice, explain its purpose, and adapt when new information appears.

How to use the Lab

  1. Watch the teaching lesson.
  2. Attempt the fictional case before opening the model.
  3. Explain the purpose of your response.
  4. Use the client reveal to reconsider.
  5. Compare, reflect, and retry.
