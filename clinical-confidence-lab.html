Four Focused Weeks

A small course with a clear job.

Each week combines short teaching, a practical tool, a fictional case lab, one small real-world experiment, and a clear stopping point.

Week 1

Why Confidence Feels Shaky

Understand what disrupts your confidence and recognize the protective pattern that follows.

Leave knowing what to practice—not just what to criticize.

Week 2

Getting Unstuck in the Room

Use a grounded clinical decision-making process when your brain suddenly has no useful suggestions.

Choose one intentional next move instead of five anxious ones.

Week 3

Lead Without Becoming Robotic

Create flexible session structure and language for focusing, redirecting, deepening, and closing.

Give the session a spine without scripting every word.

Week 4

Stop Carrying Every Session Home

Separate responsibility from total control and build a contained reflection practice.

Learn, consult when needed, and close the tab.

Questions

Before you join the list.

Is this clinical supervision?

No. This is educational mentorship and professional development. It does not replace required supervision, emergency consultation, legal guidance, workplace procedures, or licensing requirements.

How much time should I plan each week?

Approximately 60-90 minutes for the lessons, tool, case practice, implementation prompt, and reflection. The two private-session weeks require additional time.

Do I need to share real client details?

No. Fictional, composite, or fully de-identified examples are required. The course focuses on your reasoning, patterns, decisions, and support needs—not retelling a client’s private history.

What if I still feel unsure after four weeks?

You will still encounter uncertainty. The goal is to have a more grounded way to respond to it, clearer language for what support you need, and practical behaviors you can continue strengthening.

Is this the same as the 12-week Intensive?

No. The Lab is a focused four-week experience with a complete, practical outcome. The deeper 12-week Intensive is planned for January 2027 and will expand into case conceptualization, complex moments, boundaries, sustainability, and clinical identity.
