Professional Disclaimer

Education, reflection, and support - not supervision or therapy.

The Confident Clinician offers professional development for therapists and developing clinicians. This page explains what the website and offerings are meant to provide, what they are not meant to provide, and where your own professional judgment still matters.

Last updated: July 1, 2026

By using this website, joining the email list, submitting a form, purchasing a workshop, downloading a resource, or participating in a program, you understand and agree that The Confident Clinician is an educational and professional development resource. It is not a substitute for individualized professional care, clinical supervision, legal advice, ethical consultation, or emergency support.

1. Educational and Informational Purposes Only

The content on this website and in any The Confident Clinician offering is provided for educational, informational, and professional development purposes only. This includes website copy, emails, workshops, programs, videos, handouts, examples, scripts, frameworks, reflection prompts, and downloadable materials.

The goal is to support learning, reflection, clinical confidence, professional identity, and grounded decision-making. The content is not intended to diagnose, treat, supervise, direct clinical care, or replace the support required by your setting, supervisor, licensing board, professional association, attorney, accountant, or medical provider.

2. Not Therapy, Medical Advice, or Crisis Support

Nothing on this website or in any related offering should be understood as therapy, mental health treatment, medical advice, diagnosis, crisis intervention, or a recommendation for any specific treatment plan.

If you need personal mental health support, medical care, or crisis help, please contact an appropriate licensed professional, crisis line, emergency service, or local resource. This website is not monitored for emergencies and should never be used to request urgent help for yourself, a client, or another person.

3. Not Clinical Supervision or Legal/Ethical Advice

The Confident Clinician may discuss clinical themes, therapy concepts, boundaries, documentation, consultation, private practice realities, ethical considerations, and professional growth. Those discussions are general education only.

Participation does not replace clinical supervision, board-approved supervision, employer guidance, legal advice, ethics consultation, risk management support, tax advice, financial advice, or consultation from someone who knows the specific facts of your situation.

4. No Professional Relationship Is Created

Using this website, reading content, joining an email list, submitting a form, purchasing a workshop, downloading materials, or participating in an offering does not create a therapist-client relationship, supervisor-supervisee relationship, consultant-client relationship, attorney-client relationship, physician-patient relationship, or any other licensed professional relationship.

Any professional relationship must be created separately through a clear agreement, informed consent process, or written contract when appropriate.

5. Your Professional Responsibility

You are responsible for your own clinical judgment, documentation, consultation, supervision, scope of practice, ethical decision-making, employment requirements, licensure requirements, and compliance with applicable laws and professional standards.

  • Use your own judgment before applying any idea, script, framework, or example.
  • Seek supervision, consultation, legal advice, tax advice, or medical advice when needed.
  • Follow your licensing board, professional code of ethics, workplace policies, and local laws.
  • Do not share identifying client information in forms, emails, workshops, chats, or group spaces unless it is legally and ethically appropriate to do so.

6. Health, Movement, Mindfulness, and Body-Based Practices

Some content may reference mindfulness, nervous system awareness, embodiment, breath, movement, grounding, or body-based practices. This content is educational and reflective. It is not medical advice, physical therapy, yoga therapy, psychiatric care, trauma treatment, or a substitute for individualized care from a qualified provider.

Please use discretion and consult an appropriate professional before beginning or changing any practice related to your mental health, physical health, trauma history, movement, exercise, medication, or medical care.

7. No Guarantees

The Confident Clinician is designed to support professional development, reflection, confidence, clarity, and sustainability. We cannot guarantee any specific outcome, including clinical outcomes, client outcomes, income, licensure results, employment results, private practice growth, reduced anxiety, reduced burnout, or any other personal or professional result.

Your results depend on many factors, including your training, setting, supervision, licensure status, effort, client population, caseload, workplace, jurisdiction, and how you choose to apply the material.

8. Examples, Scripts, and Testimonials

Any examples, scripts, scenarios, case-style discussions, testimonials, or stories are provided for illustration and education. They are not instructions for every client, every clinician, every practice setting, or every legal or ethical situation.

Examples may be fictionalized, combined, altered, or de-identified to protect privacy. Testimonials reflect individual experiences and do not guarantee that you will have the same experience.
